How Do Members Communicate?

Communication Within Bible Study Groups

Most communication within a Bible study group takes place during meetings. The leader usually picks a passage from the Bible to discuss and read with each other. After reading the passage, the leader might ask questions about what it means or how it applies to the everyday lives of the members. This gives everyone an opportunity to participate and share the understanding they have of the passage.

Members are able to communicate with each other outside of their meetings. For example, some groups might have a group chat where members can share prayer requests or remind each other about upcoming meetings. The leader might provide study guides also, that help the members prepare for future meetings.

When participating in a discussion, you should be respectful of what other members have to say. There could be times when another member interprets a passage differently than you do. Instead of immediately disagreeing with them though, you could ask them why they interpret the passage that way.

If you are a new member and are uncomfortable speaking in front of the group, it is completely fine to listen to other members to begin with. But as you become more comfortable, you should begin participating with the discussion like asking questions and sharing your own thoughts.
