Common Bible Study Terms and Their Meanings

Understanding Bible Study Vocabulary

While attending a Bible study group for the first time, you might hear members use certain words that would be unfamiliar to anybody outside of the group. Members who have been studying the Bible for a while usually understand these terms without needing someone to explain them. This vocabulary makes it easier for already existing members to communicate with each other during their discussions.

For example, a member might refer to the Old Testament as "OT" or the New Testament as "NT." Someone who has never attended a Bible study before might not immediately understand what these abbreviations mean.

Below are some common terms that you might hear when attending a Bible study.

Common terms and their meanings

Term Meaning
Scripture Sacred writings found in the Bible.
Fellowship Spending time with other believers and building relationships.
Salvation Being saved from sin.
Parable A story used to teach a moral or spiritual lesson.
Communion A religious practice that remembers Jesus Christ's sacrifice.
OT Old Testament.
NT New Testament.
Prayer Request Asking others to pray for someone or something.
Testimony Sharing a personal experience about your faith.

Closing paragraph

As a new member, you are not expected to understand the vocabulary used within the group. If another member uses a term that is unfamiliar to you, you should feel comfortable asking them what it means. As you continue attending the meetings, you will become more familiar with the vocabulary and have an easier time following the discussions.
